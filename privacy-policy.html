Privacy Policy for Merge Infinity

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Merge Infinity ("the App").

Merge Infinity is a mobile game supported in part by advertising. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be collected and used while you play the game.

1. Information We Collect

Merge Infinity does not intentionally request sensitive personal information such as your name, home address, phone number, or financial information simply to play the game.

However, third-party services used by the App may automatically collect certain technical and usage information. This may include:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device type and operating system
  • App interactions
  • Advertising interactions
  • Approximate location derived from an IP address
  • Crash and diagnostic information

2. Advertising

Merge Infinity uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information about your device and your interactions with advertisements in order to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising services where permitted by law and your consent choices.

You can learn more about how Google handles information at:

Google Privacy Policy

3. Rewarded Ads

Merge Infinity may allow users to voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements in exchange for in-game rewards such as virtual coins.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional. Players may choose whether or not to watch available rewarded ads.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Merge Infinity may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent or present privacy choices before certain advertising technologies are used.

Users in applicable regions may be given options regarding personalized advertising, non-personalized advertising, and the use of certain advertising technologies.

Where available, users may also access privacy options to review or modify their consent choices.

5. Third-Party Services

Merge Infinity may use third-party services that operate under their own privacy policies.

Current third-party services may include:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

  • Google Play Services

These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

Merge Infinity does not sell your personal information.

Certain information may be transmitted to third-party service providers such as Google when necessary to provide advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, diagnostics, security, and related services.

Information collected directly by third-party services is retained according to the policies and retention practices of those service providers.

Merge Infinity is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.
